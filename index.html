New here? How to read this section

The one-word scale
Every indicator is boiled down to: Oversold → Undervalued → Fair value → Cautious → Overbought. Left = historically a good time to buy, right = historically a time for caution.

Phase
The big card combines that score with the trend (is price above its 200-day average and the short-term holder cost basis?) to name a phase, like “Early bull” or “Late bull”.

Cost basis
The average price a group of holders paid. Price above it = that group is in profit; below = at a loss. Short-term holders (STH) held under about 155 days; long-term holders (LTH) over 155 days.
