New here? How to read this

The scale
Oversold → Undervalued → Fair value → Cautious → Overbought. Left has historically been a good time to buy; right, a time for caution.

Phase
The summary card combines that score with the trend to name a phase, like “Early bull” or “Late bull”.

Cost basis
The average price a group of holders paid. Price above it = they’re in profit; below = at a loss. Short-term holders (STH) held under ~155 days; long-term holders (LTH) over.
