New here? How to read this section

Cost basis
The average price a group of holders paid. If today’s price is above it, that group is in profit; below it, they’re at a loss. Groups at a loss tend to sell rallies; groups in profit tend to hold.

Short-term vs long-term holders
Short-term holders (STH) held their coins under about 155 days: recent, more emotional buyers. Long-term holders (LTH) held over 155 days: patient holders who rarely sell.

Colors
Blue = cheap/cold · green = healthy · yellow = warm · orange = hot · red = overheated.

Tap any card
You get a plain-English explanation, the math behind today’s number, and what each color zone means.
